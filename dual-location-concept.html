The two houses

One brand, two climates.
Sea level and cloud level.

Six hundred miles apart, and built for opposite instincts. The Lagoon House is for the city at night; The Plateau House is for the morning before anyone else is awake. The service, the linen and the standard do not change — only the weather at the window.
